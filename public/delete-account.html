Delete your account

Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version: you can delete your account and everything we store for it, in the app or by writing to us.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Simple Bible Memory and sign in if you aren't already.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account and backup.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in with Google again.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app, write to amyruth.rubio@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your backup: your decks, cards, notes, study schedule and study settings stored in your account.
  • Every deck you published (it disappears from Discover).
  • Your sign-in: your Google account link, email and name stored in Firebase Authentication.

What is not deleted

  • The decks, cards and history already stored on your own device: delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Copies of your published decks that other people already imported into their own app.
  • Anonymous reports about decks, which are not linked to any account.

To keep a copy of your data before you delete, use Export everything in Settings. More detail is in our privacy policy.
