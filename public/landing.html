How it works

  1. Add your versesOne at a time, or a whole passage at once.
  2. Study a little each dayRate how well you remembered each one.
  3. Remember it for goodSpaced repetition brings each verse back at the right time.

Study it your way

  • FlashcardsSee the reference and recall the passage, or the other way around.
  • Type itWrite the verse and see exactly what you got right.
  • Fill in the blanksChoose from a word bank, at three difficulty levels.
  • SpeakStudy hands-free: say your answer out loud and rate yourself by voice.

Simple and private

  • No account needed. Your verses stay on your device.
  • No ads, no tracking.
  • Sign in with Google only if you want a backup on other devices.
  • Share decks with others, or discover decks they have shared.
  • In English and Spanish, and works with screen readers.

Speak mode uses your phone's own speech recognition, which may need an internet connection on some phones.
