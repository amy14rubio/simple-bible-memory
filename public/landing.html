A message from the developer

The idea for this app really came from countless hours of memorizing Bible verses on Anki, and wishing there was something designed specifically for Scripture memorization.

Every feature you’ll find here was inspired by something I wished I had during my own Bible memorization journey. I wanted to make something that would make the process a little more enjoyable and accessible.

I hope this app can be a simple, helpful little tool for you to use!

Thanks for stopping by, and God bless! ❤︎
