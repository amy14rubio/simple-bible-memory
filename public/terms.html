Bible translations

With Autofill, the app fetches the text of Bible translations from YouVersion. That text belongs to its publishers and is shown with their notice, for your personal study only. You may not copy it out of the app in bulk or share it. The app does not back it up, export it or publish it, and a translation may be switched off at any time, for example if its publisher asks.

Community rules for public collections

A published collection must not contain or link to:

  • sexually explicit content, or graphic violence;
  • anything illegal, or that promotes illegal activity or harm;
  • spam, advertising, or anything for sale;
  • personal information about other people, or content that impersonates someone;
  • text you do not have the right to share: a public collection publishes the text only of open (public-domain or freely licensed) Bible translations, or your own words. For a copyrighted translation such as the NIV or ESV, only the verse references are published, never its text;
  • content that is not about Scripture and memorising it.

Reporting and removing content

Anyone can report a public collection from the flag button on its page, or by writing to us. We review reports, and we may hide or remove any collection, and stop an account from publishing, if a collection breaks these rules or if a copyright owner asks. We try to act quickly, but we do not review every collection before it is published.
