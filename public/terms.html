Using the app

Simple Bible Memory ("the app", "we") helps you memorise Bible verses. By using the app you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it. You must be at least 13 years old to sign in.

Your decks and your data

The decks and cards you create are yours. Most of them stay on your device. Our privacy policy explains what is stored and when.

Publishing a deck

You can publish a deck so that others can find it in Discover and import a copy. When you publish, you keep ownership of your deck, and you give us permission to store it and show it, and to let anyone read and import a copy of it, for as long as it is published. You can unpublish it at any time.

Community rules for public decks

A published deck must not contain or link to:

  • hateful, harassing, threatening or abusive content, including content that attacks people because of who they are;
  • sexually explicit content, or graphic violence;
  • anything illegal, or that promotes illegal activity or harm;
  • spam, advertising, or anything for sale;
  • personal information about other people, or content that impersonates someone;
  • text you do not have the right to share: public decks may use only open (public-domain or freely licensed) Bible translations, or your own words, so copyrighted translations such as the NIV or ESV cannot be published;
  • content that is not about Scripture and memorising it.

Reporting and removing content

Anyone can report a public deck from the flag button on its page, or by writing to us. We review reports, and we may hide or remove any deck, and stop an account from publishing, if a deck breaks these rules or if a copyright owner asks. We try to act quickly, but we do not review every deck before it is published.
