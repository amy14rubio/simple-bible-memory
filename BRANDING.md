The **source code** is open under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **brand** is not part of that license.

The following belong to the maintainer and are **not** licensed for reuse:

- The name **Simple Bible Memory** (and **Memoria Bíblica Simple**).
- The logo and app icons (`public/logo.svg`, the favicons, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/og-image.jpg`, and the Android and iOS app icon files).
- The Google Play and App Store listings, screenshots and store text, and the website at simple-bible-memory.web.app.
- The privacy policy, terms and delete-account pages (`public/privacy-policy.html` and friends). They describe the maintainer's own service. A fork must write its own.

## If you fork the project

You are welcome to fork it, change it, and publish your own version, as long as you follow the AGPL (your source stays open under the same license, with the copyright notices kept) and you:

1. Use **a different name** and **a different icon**, so people can tell your app from this one.
2. Create **your own** Firebase project, Google Play and Apple developer accounts, and API keys. Never reuse this project's identifiers (bundle ids, `.firebaserc`, Apple Team ID).
3. Write your own privacy policy and terms.
4. Do not say or imply that your version is made, endorsed or supported by this project.

Pull requests to this repository are of course welcome, and they do not need a new name.
